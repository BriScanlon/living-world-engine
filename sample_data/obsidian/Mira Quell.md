# Mira Quell

A sharp-eyed fence who moves stolen goods for [[Sela Marrow]] out of the back room of [[The Gilded Gull]].
She knows about [[The Blackfen Hag]]'s bargains and pays the hag in silver every new moon.

Mira Quell fears the Crown Envoy. Mira Quell owes a debt to Wenna Tull.
